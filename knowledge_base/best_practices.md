# Best Practices

## Voters
- Confirm the voter ID and polling station details before asking an officer to review a record.
- Keep personal information private and do not share voter details in public channels.
- Ask an authorised officer when the displayed information does not match your documents.

## Polling officers
- Verify the exact voter ID and returned record details before taking action.
- Record discrepancies in neutral language and preserve the original details.
- Escalate inconsistencies through the authorised review procedure; do not infer eligibility or create records.
